Chapter 1

A Letter From Your Hosts

Hi friends,

You're getting this because you're a leader in your own world. You build community, hold space, make art, or carry a big vision for a better world, and you're actually making it real.

That makes you a camp counselor. Each of you runs a “camp” of your own: a community, a circle, a practice, a movement.

We keep meeting incredible people like you, and we want more ways to connect and build the future together.

So we're curious. What happens when we bring all of you together in one place? What kind of collective intelligence wakes up? We don't know yet. That's the exciting part.

It's an experiment: birthing the new tools and rituals for the future we're already creating.

Call it surviving to thriving. We want to play with emergent ways of strengthening our living network.

And there's no better way to go deeper together than summer camp. Play. Real connection. The kind of bonding that happens around a fire.

It's been two years since our last real camp gathering. For some of you, this is a reunion. For many, it's your first time on this land. Either way, we can't wait.

Big tree hugs,
Julian, Nathaniel & Sherene

Hearts × a thousand to Sherene, for making this all possible.

Who's Invited

Community builders · Space holders · Facilitators · Healers · Creatives · Visionaries

You spend your days stewarding other people's growth. You set up the circle, read the room and make sure everyone else gets what they came for.

Now imagine a place where you get to be your full self. Where you experiment, play and get pushed to your own edges, the way you usually do for everyone else.

This weekend, we hold each other.

We get to take up space and explore new places of vulnerability, among people who celebrate the full human experience. Shadows and triggers aren't something to hide here. They're the fastest path to deeper connection, growth and freedom, to waking up our gifts and stepping into our wholeness. And that's where the real fun starts.

Counselor's note

If you've been feeling new downloads coming in lately, you're not imagining it. Bring your field notes. We'll compare them by the fire.

Chapter 3

Remember Our Magic

Everyone is talking about collective intelligence. Usually that means trading ideas and finding the middle. We think it runs much deeper. With the right spark, it comes alive in a room in a way none of us has seen yet.

Hover or tapTap a circle.

The magic of the land

The redwoods are antennas. The coastal fog blurs the realms. Something potent here wakes us up in magical, unexpected ways.

The magic of each of us

The gifts, practices and strange brilliance every person carries in.

The magic of the container

A shared intention, a few agreements, and a weekend held with care.

Where they meet

Collective magic from the relational field: from each of us, out to each other, into the land. Here we birth the rituals of the future.

Our practices, in the moment.

At Spirit Camp, practices usually come in blocks: two hours of breathwork, two of ecstatic dance, an hour of authentic relating.

Now so many of us carry hundreds of hours of practice. What if we met each moment with what it needs?

  • The circle gets stuck in talk. Someone starts a beat, and the energy moves again.
  • The dance is roaring, but part of the room feels left behind. We slow down, tune in, and bring everyone along, or open a second space.
  • Someone has never been to an ecstatic dance. Surrounded by fifteen people who have, their body gets it in minutes, not years.

Our practices aren't only for healing the past. They're for living this moment, fully, together.

The campy and the sacred, in the same circle.

Chapter 5

The Shape of the Weekend

Expect camp favorites: authentic relating games, forest walks, dancing and songs around the fire. Expect a few things we've never tried, too. And if the moment calls for it, we might stop everything and change the plan.

Field tip

Think of it as improv camp.

We'll play with different models of facilitation that help us move as one and create together. We bring a little structure and a few prompts, and see what magic unfolds.

  1. Know each other deeply

    From Friday's opening circle on, we want to see how much trust and belonging we can build. This is the kind of group that can go there. Come ready to go deep, bare your soul and have a lot of fun.

  2. The land, and play

    We connect with the land. Time in the redwoods with music, and the first rounds of co-created, improvised play.

  3. Weave our gifts

    Short talks from friends doing cool things, then an emergent, experimental container where we weave our collective gifts together in real time. This is where the three circles meet.

  4. Celebrate

    We celebrate our first 24 hours together with ceremony, fun and probably a little dance.

  5. Land it, carry it out

    We come back together to integrate, so the magic fully lands in our bodies. Then we set our intentions for carrying it, and our newly activated counselor skills, out into the world.

Camp keeps its own clock.
